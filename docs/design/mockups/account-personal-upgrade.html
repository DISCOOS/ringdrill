DESIGN-015 §5.3 and §5.9. A personal account is the same form, minus the one section it has nothing to put in: Members arrives with the organisation, not before it. The handle does not wait — every account that publishes wants a readable URL, so Details offers one suggested from the name and claimed explicitly, first-come and globally unique. The upgrade sheet states the part users get wrong before the action: it cannot be undone by removing the member.

The handle is the short name in your plans’ web addresses — ringdrill.app/d/kari-gulbrandsen/winter-drill — and the name you give somebody so they can share a plan with you. Until one is claimed your plans publish under the account’s id, which works, but is not a name anybody can pass on.
Claimed first-come and globally unique, like a handle anywhere else. Changing it later keeps the old links working — the previous name redirects (ADR-0074 §2).

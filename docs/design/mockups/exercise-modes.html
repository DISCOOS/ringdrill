9 · Changing mode on an exercise that already has one. The mode decides team-to-post assignment, so it is a real change and it says so.

AVBRYTBYTT

The consequence is stated as the two numbers the author cares about, computed before they commit.

What the author types, in total

  • One mode per exercise — a choice from three, defaulting to today’s behaviour.
  • An Øvingstid on a station, only where it differs from the exercise’s.
  • A parallel group, only in Delt, only where posts genuinely run together.

No round list, no per-round entries, no clock faces. Every time in this mockup is derived, and the uniform exercise — most of a course day — is untouched by all of it.

Compared with ADR-0062’s rounds: list

rounds: asks the author to translate what they know (“post b takes 100 minutes”) into round-space (“round 2 is 100 minutes”) by hand, and to enumerate every round to say one thing about one of them. In the editor that is an unbounded list with inheritance rules to explain. Here the two facts are stated where they already live, and the round structure falls out of them.

What it costs

  • Mode and per-station duration reach the archive. Neither is recoverable from the derived schedule, so this needs an ADR-0059 migration rung — where a timing-only rounds: would have needed none.
  • Rotation math changes rather than accumulates. Mode alters team-to-station assignment, which is the part ADR-0062 flagged as the half that grows.
  • Waiting becomes visible. Panel 5 is honest, not comfortable: an author who did not realise their posts were unequal will see idle time they did not know they had.

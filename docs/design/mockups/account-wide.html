DESIGN-015 §5.6. On wide screens and on the web the account page follows the master/detail model from ADR-0030: the member list is master and the selected member is detail, not a bottom sheet. The account switcher sits in the top bar, and must also appear in the publish dialog so nobody publishes to the wrong account without seeing it.

Copy is shown in en. The app ships nb as well; where the exact Norwegian wording is part of the decision, DESIGN-015 quotes it inline.

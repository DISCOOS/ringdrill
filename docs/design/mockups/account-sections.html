DESIGN-015 §5.6. One form, not two. Profile is always present and edits the user — name, nickname, phone; Details and Members edit the account and appear only for an owner. The two panels are the same screen seen from the two sides of that rule: an owner of an organisation, and a member of one. Sections a role cannot use are absent rather than disabled — a greyed-out row invites a question the screen cannot answer.

Copy is shown in en. The app ships nb as well; where the exact Norwegian wording is part of the decision, DESIGN-015 quotes it inline.
